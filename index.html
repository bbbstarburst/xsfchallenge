The Route

Across the city, twice

The XSF Challenge traces two distinct legs across San Francisco — from the Golden Gate Bridge south through the neighborhoods to Candlestick, then back north from Lake Merced to the Embarcadero. The gap between legs is a vehicle transfer — part of the route, not a shortcut.

Leg 1 — GGB South → Candlestick
Leg 2 — Lake Merced → Embarcadero
Leg 1

GGB South → Candlestick

Starting at the south anchorage of the Golden Gate Bridge, the first leg traces a path through the Richmond, Inner Sunset, and Bayview neighborhoods to Candlestick Point.

~16mi
Leg 2

Lake Merced → Embarcadero

The return leg begins at Lake Merced and winds north through the Sunset, Western Addition, and Financial District to finish at the Embarcadero waterfront.

~18mi

Year Two · 2027

Saturday, April 10, 2027

The second XSF crossing is confirmed. Same format: start at the bridge before sunrise, finish at the Embarcadero after dark. The annual group outing is personally organized and invitation-based — a small group of friends, not an open race.

01

Invitation-based

The annual group crossing is personally organized. It's a small, privately assembled group — not an open registration event. No entry fees, no bibs, no timing chips.

02

Self-supported

No aid stations, no crew. Plan your nutrition and gear like any backcountry objective — but you're in the city, so corner stores and water fountains are fair game.

03

Open to anyone independently

The routes are public and permanent. Anyone can walk or run the Crosstown Trail and Double Cross any time, with or without the annual group. Good navigation resources are online.

04

A tradition worth repeating

The goal is a repeatable annual event that deepens friendships and creates something with a future — not an organization, not a production, not another job.
